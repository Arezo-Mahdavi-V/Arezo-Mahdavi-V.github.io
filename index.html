About me

Hi, welcome to my world! I'm Arezo, a materials scientist and PhD student at Penn State. I work at the intersection of high-temperature molten-salt electrochemistry, pyrometallurgy, energy materials, and advanced materials and device characterization. My current research focus aims to transform ironmaking into a low‑energy, low‑emission cornerstone of a greener world.


I enjoy turning tough challenges into innovative ideas and real-world solutions. I'm a hands-on researcher who believes in getting my hands dirty—always with gloves and proper PPE, of course. My work is grounded in practical lab experience, precise measurements, disciplined log-keeping, traceable sample tracking, and rigorous experimental protocols.

Outside the lab, you'll find me playing tennis (sometimes with a fluffy poodle nearby), skiing down snowy slopes, or capturing the night sky through my camera lens. If you're interested in my research—or we simply share a hobby—let's connect and build something meaningful together.
